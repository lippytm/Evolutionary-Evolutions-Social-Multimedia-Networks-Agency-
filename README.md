## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Review the canonical CEIU and product status in Prompt #11.
2. Confirm the proposed channel use is permitted by the source product's approval scope.
3. Create a Campaign Passport.
4. Preserve truth labels, fictional boundaries, material limitations, rights, accessibility, and correction links in every content unit.
5. Use only approved offers and affiliate language.
6. Test the complete customer journey, including accessible forms, billing disclosures, cancellation, refunds, and support.
7. Obtain HumanApprovalGate before launch.
8. Monitor outcomes and propagate corrections across every active channel.

## Stop-work conditions

Stop or quarantine delivery when:

- the source product is not approved for the proposed use;
- truth labels or fictional disclosures are removed;
- restricted data is exposed;
- rights are missing, disputed, expired, or revoked;
- marketing makes unsupported operational, earnings, funding, investment, health, legal, or environmental claims;
- a critical accessibility, privacy, security, safety, or rights defect exists;
- a material correction has not propagated;
- HumanApprovalGate is missing, expired, or revoked;
- RiskGate becomes Red.
