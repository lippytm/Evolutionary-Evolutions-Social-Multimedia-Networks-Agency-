# P-011-CRM-001 — Social Publishing, Audience Journey, and Campaign QA System

**Canonical source:** `lippytm/Prompt-11-`  
**Status:** Q2 social-delivery mirror

## Purpose

Connect the Prompt #11 CRM to human-reviewed Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, Substack, and future-platform content while preserving identity disclosure, consent, truth labels, rights, accessibility, attribution, corrections, and HumanApprovalGate.

## Staged platform assets

The social-delivery system may prepare:

- profile headline or biography;
- About section;
- launch and project-update posts;
- articles and newsletters;
- carousel, image, audio, video, and interactive briefs;
- calls to action;
- comments, support, and human-handoff plans;
- correction and retirement notices.

Preparation does not equal publication. A human must approve and publish through an authorized account session unless a separately certified provider adapter exists.

## Audience journey

`discover → understand → choose_preferences → learn_or_engage → request_support_or_offer → human_review → active_relationship → feedback → success_review → correction_or_retirement`

## Content Passport

Every asset records:

- content ID and version;
- platform and asset type;
- intended audience role and journey stage;
- source and claim references;
- fact, assessment, hypothesis, speculative-design, or fiction labels;
- creator, AI assistance, and fictional-character disclosure;
- rights and trademark status;
- accessibility assets: alt text, captions, transcript, reading level, and touch/navigation review;
- call to action and destination;
- consent requirements for any follow-up;
- approval, publish URL, metrics, correction, and retirement status.

## Cross-platform mutation rules

A content mutation must preserve:

- core meaning and material limitations;
- AI and fiction disclosures;
- source and date context;
- prohibited outcome guarantees;
- rights and accessibility requirements;
- correction and supersession links;
- campaign and CRM attribution identifiers.

Changing format does not authorize a stronger claim.

## Campaign Passport

Required fields:

- campaign ID, owner, objective, audience, and duration;
- approved source content and product version;
- channel list and budget;
- consent and suppression rules;
- attribution model: estimated, modeled, or confirmed;
- accessibility and environmental plan;
- customer or learner support plan;
- success, pause, and stop-work criteria;
- correction and retirement procedures;
- HumanApprovalGate.

## CRM integration

Social events may create bounded CRM signals such as content viewed, voluntary inquiry, subscription, support request, lesson interest, or opt-out. They may not silently create sensitive profiles, merge identities, or authorize messaging on another channel.

## Prohibited actions

- profile or post publication without approval;
- automated direct messaging without consent;
- uploading audiences or contact data without lawful purpose and provider review;
- deceptive human or clone impersonation;
- unsupported earnings, funding, investment, health, legal, or environmental claims;
- hidden affiliate relationships;
- using CRM attribution as accounting proof;
- publishing private or restricted evidence.

## Quality gates

- TruthGate
- DateGate
- IdentityDisclosureGate
- RightsGate
- PrivacyGate
- CommunicationGate
- AccessibilityGate
- FairnessGate
- AttributionGate
- RevenueIntegrityGate
- CorrectionGate
- HumanApprovalGate

## Current boundary

Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, and Substack drafts are staged in Airtable and a downloadable activation pack. No live social profile modification or publication is claimed by this repository mirror.
