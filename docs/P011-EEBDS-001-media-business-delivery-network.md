**Status:** Architecture specification v0.3  

## Purpose

The Evolutionary Evolutions Social Multimedia Networks Agency is the distribution and audience-learning layer for the **Encyclopedia Educational Entertainment and Business of Businesses Delivery System**.

It transforms certified or explicitly labeled pilot CEIUs into coordinated media packages without weakening their evidence labels, fictional boundaries, privacy protections, rights restrictions, accessibility requirements, environmental commitments, corrections, or HumanApprovalGate.

The agency does not convert an idea into an approved product merely by publishing it. Distribution status and certification status remain separate.

## Delivery covenant

> **Deliver the story without losing the lesson. Deliver the offer without exaggerating the evidence. Deliver the character without impersonating the creator. Deliver the NFT without exposing private data. Deliver the business without promising guaranteed outcomes.**

## Audience journey

Every campaign shall support one or more stages:

1. **Discover** — encounter the character, question, or ecosystem.
2. **Understand** — receive a clear Reality Mode explanation.
3. **Question** — apply IF, MAYBE, WHY NOT, and DON'T DO THAT.
4. **Experience** — follow the story, simulation, or performance.
5. **Build** — complete a project, exercise, or business experiment.
6. **Verify** — inspect sources, tests, limitations, rights, and corrections.
7. **Choose** — decide whether to buy, join, contribute, license, affiliate, or decline.
8. **Learn-to-Earn** — receive compensation only under an approved, transparent contribution program.
9. **Improve** — submit feedback, defects, corrections, and accessibility findings.
10. **Steward** — help preserve or responsibly retire the product and its community.

## Content atomization rules

A long-form product may be divided into smaller media units only when each unit preserves enough context to avoid misleading the audience.

Every atomized unit must retain:

- product and version identity;
- relevant truth label;
- fiction or simulation disclosure;
- material limitation;
- source or full-context link where required;
- correction reference;
- accessibility alternative;
- rights-cleared media assets;
- call-to-action consistent with the approved offer.

A dramatic clip cannot remove the disclosure that a technology is fictional or experimental. A social post cannot transform an allegation into a fact. A trailer cannot imply guaranteed income, investment return, medical outcome, funding, or operational capability.

## Character presentation

Characters may serve as guides, comedians, investigators, builders, witnesses, entrepreneurs, environmental stewards, historians, or red-team critics.

Character media must:

- identify the work as fictional when extraordinary powers or events are involved;
- distinguish Charles Earl Lipshay from Lippy Killjoy, lippytm, lippytm.AI, synthetic voices, avatars, and AI interfaces;
- avoid fabricated personal memories or statements attributed to the real creator;
- use only approved voice, likeness, image, and performance rights;
- preserve the character's limitations, consent rules, and correction mechanisms;
- avoid copied third-party designs, dialogue, insignia, plots, or distinctive lore.

## Educational Entertainment packaging

Every media package should include a balanced set of components:

1. **Hook** — a character, mystery, conflict, or surprising question.
2. **Reality capsule** — the minimum factual context necessary for understanding.
3. **Story experience** — an original fictional or dramatized sequence.
4. **Quantum Questions** — IF, MAYBE, WHY NOT, and DON'T DO THAT.
5. **Build invitation** — a safe, bounded project or exercise.
6. **Business application** — a transparent offer or opportunity to create value.
7. **Quality card** — status, limitations, risks, corrections, and next review.
8. **Stewardship invitation** — feedback, accessibility reporting, correction, and community contribution.

## Business of Businesses campaign architecture

### Offer ladder

Campaigns may support:

- $1 Evidence Processing Field Guide;
- $2 Affiliate Edition;
- $7 Master Evidence and Product Ledger;
- certified ebooks, audiobooks, video books, and interactive products;
- workbooks, courses, games, toolkits, and project kits;
- memberships and Learning-to-Earning programs;
- creator, educator, affiliate, and business licenses;
- certified franchise-node packages;
- institutional and enterprise services.

Prices are design inputs and may change. No offer may imply guaranteed income, funding, investment return, health outcome, legal outcome, or employment.

### Affiliate integrity

Affiliates must receive a controlled kit containing:

- approved product description;
- required disclosures;
- permitted and prohibited claims;
- current pricing and terms;
- audience eligibility;
- privacy and data-handling rules;
- approved media assets;
- correction and withdrawal procedure;
- version and expiration date.

Affiliates may not edit away material limitations or present a Q0–Q3 item as a certified Q4 product.

### Membership and community integrity

Memberships must state:

- what is included;
- billing frequency;
- cancellation process;
- content and support limits;
- data practices;
- community rules;
- compensation rules for contributions;
- correction and moderation procedures.

## Accessibility delivery requirements

Applicable media shall include:

- captions;
- transcripts;
- audio description planning;
- readable type and contrast;
- keyboard-accessible interactions;
- plain-language alternatives;
- screen-reader-compatible documents;
- accessible forms and payment paths;
- flexible pacing;
- content warnings when materially relevant.

Accessibility defects are product defects, not optional enhancements.

## Environmental delivery requirements

Campaign planning shall consider:

- production energy and material use;
- travel and physical-event impacts;
- hosting, storage, and streaming choices;
- blockchain and NFT resource use;
- physical merchandise and packaging;
- device and bandwidth accessibility;
- unsupported environmental marketing claims.

Where practical, the agency shall prefer efficient delivery and disclose material tradeoffs rather than claiming perfect sustainability.

## Metrics

The agency shall measure:

- reach and qualified engagement;
- learning completion;
- fact-versus-fiction comprehension;
- build-project completion;
- accessibility failures;
- privacy and security incidents;
- customer satisfaction;
- refunds and chargebacks;
- support burden;
- affiliate compliance;
- correction response time;
- environmental delivery indicators;
- lawful revenue;
- community and human benefit.

High reach or revenue cannot compensate for failed critical gates.

## Stop-work conditions

Stop or quarantine delivery when:

- the source product is not approved for the proposed use;
- a truth label or fictional boundary is removed;
- private or restricted data is exposed;
- rights or permissions are missing, expired, disputed, or revoked;
- an offer includes unsupported operational, income, funding, investment, health, legal, or environmental claims;
- an affiliate or channel materially misrepresents the product;
- a critical accessibility, privacy, security, safety, or rights defect is found;
- a correction is not propagated to active channels;
- HumanApprovalGate is absent, expired, or revoked;
- RiskGate becomes Red.
