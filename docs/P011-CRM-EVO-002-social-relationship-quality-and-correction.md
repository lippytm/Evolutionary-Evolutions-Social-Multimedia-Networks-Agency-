# P-011-CRM-EVO-002 — Social Relationship Quality and Correction System

This Social Multimedia Agency mirror connects canonical Prompt #11 module `P-011-CRM-EVO-002` to approved Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, Threads, Substack, and future-platform content workflows.

## Social CRM contract

Every public asset receives a Content Passport with source, truth/fiction label, audience, purpose, platform, campaign, CTA, rights, accessibility assets, version, approval, publication URL, metrics, correction route, and retirement state.

CRM signals may inform aggregate content planning and customer/learner support. They may not be used for hidden sensitive profiling, unconsented direct messages, silent contact enrichment, political manipulation, or automated high-impact decisions.

## Content mutation

One human-approved source may become platform-specific editions only after checking:

- factual and fiction boundaries;
- rights and attribution;
- platform length and format;
- accessible captions, alt text, transcripts, and contrast;
- disclosures and calls to action;
- consent and suppression boundaries;
- source and campaign provenance;
- correction and supersession propagation;
- HumanApprovalGate.

## Quality telemetry

Record reach, engagement, learner/customer actions, complaints, accessibility findings, rights concerns, corrections, support needs, attribution confidence, and environmental delivery metrics primarily in aggregate.

## Stop-work

Stop publication or outreach when source, rights, privacy, consent, accessibility, provider permission, security, correction, or HumanApprovalGate fails.

No profile was modified and no post was published by this architecture mirror. Publication remains a human-controlled provider-specific action.
